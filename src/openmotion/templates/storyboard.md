# Film brief and storyboard

## Brief
State the audience, one-sentence visual thesis, target duration, aspect ratio,
and whether the film needs narration, music, or silent playback.

## Sources and claims
List source documents/versions and the facts each supports. Distinguish measured
results, illustrative examples, and proposed future behavior. Store approved
portable assets in `assets/`; this project should not depend on another checkout.

## Scenes
For each scene, specify: purpose, duration, on-screen copy, moving objects,
what changes and why, reading hold, and transition to the next scene.
Use the visual mechanism appropriate to the idea; do not force every story
into a pipeline or a network.

## Review notes
Record scientific/content corrections and visual issues from the preview.
Check labels, typography, margins, timing, provenance, and audio before final export.
